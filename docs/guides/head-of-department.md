# Head of department guide

**Approvals → Results** lists submitted tests, urgent first. Expand a row to see each result, its limits, pass or fail, the working behind calculated values, attachments, and who did the test.

- **Approve** locks the result. Nobody can change it after that, except through a report rejection sent back for re-testing.
- **Reject** returns the test to the analyst with your reason on their screen.
- **Approve selected:** tick several. Any that fail are listed with the reason; the rest still go through.

You can't approve results you entered yourself. Those show a note, and need someone else, unless your Admin has relaxed this for a very small lab.

When every active test on a sample is approved, a draft report appears for review.

**Analytics** shows rejection rates and reasons, and workload by technician and instrument.
