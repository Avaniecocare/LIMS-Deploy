# Management guide

Your access is read-only.

## Dashboard

Choose a date range. **Every number is a link** to the records behind it, and matches the report it links to.

- **Operations:** samples received, status, tests pending by stage, TAT crossed, urgent, on hold, TAT compliance
- **Quality:** reports awaiting review and approval, rejection rate and reasons, revisions issued
- **People and equipment:** workload and output, upcoming calibrations
- **Commercial:** quotations sent, conversion rate, billed against collected, outstanding and ageing, top customers and tests

## Reports

**Reports** has 24 reports grouped by area. Filter by date, customer, department and technician where they apply. Each exports to Excel and PDF.

Roles without customer access see these reports without customer columns, and the same goes for their exports.
