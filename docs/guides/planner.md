# Lab manager and planner guide

You see job and sample codes rather than customer names. That is deliberate, to keep testing impartial.

## Planning

**Planning → Unplanned** lists tests requested but not yet planned.

1. Select one sample, or several from the same job to plan them as a group.
2. Choose the grade or specification. For **dual grade**, choose a second: results are checked against both.
3. Use the suggestions from the grade, or from a test or chemical group, then add or remove tests.
4. For chemical tests choose **Spectro** or **Wet**. Add office instructions and technical data for the technician.
5. **Plan.** Due dates come from each test's turnaround time and the working-day calendar, shorter for urgent jobs.

The limits in force are stored with each test, so later master changes don't affect work in progress.

## Assigning

**Planning → Unassigned:** select tests and choose a technician and instrument. You see each person's and instrument's current workload, and which instruments are past calibration. Reassigning works the same way from **Assigned**.

## Job cards

**Job card** on a job prints what the bench needs: tests, due dates, instructions, parameters and stages, with no customer name or prices.

## Cancelling

You can cancel tests, samples and jobs. You first see what would be affected. A reason is required, and cancelled work stays visible. Tests on an approved or issued report can't be cancelled; ask the signatory to revise the report.

## Keeping an eye on things

Reports show pending plans, TAT crossed, and performance by machine and technician. You are notified when tests cross their due date.
