# Authorised signatory guide

## Approving

**Approvals → Sign** lists reports that have passed review. Check the preview, then:

- **Approve and sign:** re-enter your password. This is your electronic signature. Your signature image, name, designation, the time and a hash of the report are recorded. The content, company details and terms are frozen at this moment.
- **Reject:** give a reason, and choose to correct the report or send tests back.

You can't sign a report you reviewed.

The report prints "Electronically approved" with your name, the time and the hash. It never says "Digitally Signed", as no certificate is applied.

## Issuing

After approving, the final PDF takes a moment. Then **Issue**, with an email address if you want it sent. The delivery log shows what happened, and you can **Email again**.

If a customer is set to have reports withheld until paid, issuing is refused until the invoice is paid.

## Revising

An approved or issued report is never edited. To correct one, **Revise** and give a reason. A new revision goes through review and approval again. On issue it states what it replaces, and the earlier version is marked superseded, but stays downloadable.

## NABL

When your deployment has NABL mode on, a report with in-scope parameters gets a ULR number at approval, and out-of-scope items print in their own section. Nothing about accreditation prints when it is off.
