# Sales and business development guide

## Customers

**Customers → New customer.** Enter the name, state and two-digit GST state code. GSTIN is optional, but it is checked: the state code must match. Credit days default from settings. Add contacts and mark one as primary, since quotations and acknowledgements go there first. Add billing and report addresses.

## Orders

**Orders** tracks enquiries from email, phone, letter or walk-in. The chips at the top show the pipeline: New, Quoted, Won, Lost. A linked quotation moves the order along by itself. Attach the customer's enquiry to the order.

## Quotations

1. **New quotation.** Choose the customer. Add a line for each sample and test.
2. **Rates are looked up for you:** the customer's rate if there is one, otherwise the standard rate. To use a different rate you need permission and must give a reason. It shows on the line as an override.
3. **Save.** The system works out subtotal, GST and the rounded total. Customers in your state get CGST and SGST; others get IGST.
4. **Send.** Enter the address and an optional message. The PDF is produced and emailed, and the sent history shows delivery.

| Status | Means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Not sent | Edit, send |
| Sent | With the customer | Accept, reject (with reason), revise |
| Accepted | Customer agreed | Convert to a job |
| Rejected or Expired | Closed | Revise |

**Revising** creates a new version and keeps the old one. A sent quotation is never changed in place. Quotations expire by themselves after the validity date, and you are notified shortly before.

**Convert to job** carries over the customer, samples, tests and rates, once only.
