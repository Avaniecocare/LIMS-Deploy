# Sample reception guide

## Booking a job

- **From an accepted quotation:** choose it from the list on the booking page. You don't see its prices.
- **Without a quotation,** for repeat customers on agreed rates: **Book samples**, give the reason, then **Book job**. It is recorded.

Fill in the customer's letter or challan details, PO, receipt mode, report details and contact. Mark **Urgent** for a shorter due date. Mark **Sample drawn by the laboratory** if the lab took the sample: the report then leaves out the "not drawn by our laboratory" statement.

For each sample give the description, condition, quantity, size and whether it is **returnable**. Add any number of custom details, such as heat number or batch. Job and sample numbers are assigned on saving.

## Deviations

Record anything wrong on arrival: damage, size or client ID not specified, insufficient quantity, improper stamp, missing test details. Add a remark. Deviations print on the SRF and the test report.

## Images and letters

- **Sample images:** on the job, use **Use camera**, or upload a file if there is no camera.
- **Customer letters:** capture each page, then **Combine pages into PDF**, or upload a PDF from a scanner.

## Documents

From the job's Documents tab:

- **Sample request form** to print
- **Acknowledgement**, which you can also email to the customer, with a delivery log
- **All labels**, with a QR code. Print one per sample and stick it on. Scanning it opens the sample.

## Hold

**Put on hold** stops work on a job. A reason is required. **Release hold** returns it to where it was.

The job's **Timeline** shows every stage, person and time.
